# Continuation writing

- Reconstruct established facts, character knowledge, relationships, unresolved promises, chronology, location, voice, and the exact continuation point from the imported text.
- In continuation mode, extend the active causal line without resetting the cast or manufacturing a new premise.
- In series mode, open a distinct conflict, place, or time horizon while preserving shared canon; establish the new dramatic engine early.
- Treat compressed source packages and retrieved passages as evidence. Keep unsupported gaps explicit rather than inventing past canon.
- New complications must grow from the established state and the user's direction. Preserve the source voice through an operational style profile without copying wording.
